Frontend app

Temperature slider and preset controls, live chart, heater state, QR scanner, PWA install, Android and Windows support.

Backend APIs

/api/status · /api/target · /api/telemetry · /api/control · /api/control/target

Wokwi hardware

DHT22 on GPIO 15, LCD SDA/SCL on 21/22, heater on 2, target buttons on 25/26.

Live synchronization

Current temperature, target, state, and heater status are shared by app and Wokwi display.

Target range

Users can set 32.5°C to 60°C using the app slider or physical +/- buttons.
